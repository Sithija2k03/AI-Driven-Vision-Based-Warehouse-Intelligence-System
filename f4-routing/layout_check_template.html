      Walking distances how routes are costed

      Each walkway is a line on the map canvas. The canvas outline is the warehouse wall, set to , so one pixel is . A walkway's length in metres follows from that, unless you have entered a measured length for it. A staircase trip, up or down, is m, the same as walking that far: it is part of the path, not a punishment. Pick two points to see the shortest walk between them and exactly which walkways it uses; it is also drawn on the floor plan above. Clicking a blue dot on the plan sets it as the destination.

      Walkway lengths

      To correct a distance, measure the walkway end to end on the floor and add it to WALKWAY_LENGTH_M in warehouse_layout.py (for example {'W1': 24.0}); every step along that walkway is stretched or shrunk to match. Stair length is STAIR_LENGTH_M; a different length for one staircase goes in STAIR_LENGTH_BY_STAIR. Then rerun build_warehouse_graph.py, build_distance_matrix.py, baselines.py and train_ppo.py.
